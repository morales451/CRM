What exactly do I copy?

The whole page. On the ZoomInfo contact, press Ctrl+A then Ctrl+C and paste it here. You don't have to select carefully — menus, buttons and the colleagues listed further down are all thrown away.

It works out which name on the page is the contact by matching it against the email address, so other people listed on the same page can't get mixed in.

Smaller selections work too. The Contact Details panel pastes like this, and the (B) / (HQ) / (D) / (M) tags are read — that's how it knows which number is the mobile:

Michael Delacruz
Director of Facilities
Harlow Enterprises
Contact Details
Emails
mdelacruz@harlowenterprises.com
(B)
Phone numbers
(713) 555-0100
(HQ)
(409) 555-0102
(M)

Copying only the Contact Details panel gives you the email and phones but no name — ZoomInfo keeps the name at the top of the page. Either grab the whole page, or type the name in the boxes below and press Read the paste; anything you've typed is kept.

These also work:

Name: Robert Chen
Job Title: Director of Facilities
Email Address: rchen@boxer.com
Direct Phone: 713-555-0199

…or a row copied straight out of a ZoomInfo export in Excel.
