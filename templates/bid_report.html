Proposal

Below is exactly what we'll do to your roof, why each step matters, and what it costs. We provide all labor, materials, tools, equipment, and insurance.

Your Project At A Glance

What we're doing: Cleaning your roof and applying a three-layer silicone coating system that seals leaks and reflects the sun's heat.
Why it helps: It adds a new, seamless waterproof layer over your existing roof, without the cost and disruption of a full tear-off.
What's protected: A {{ bid.warranty_years }}-year manufacturer warranty from Henry covering materials and labor, plus our own 2-year workmanship warranty.
Proposal good for: 14 days

Coating

  1. Clean the roof. We power wash the entire surface to remove dirt and debris. Coatings only bond to a clean surface, so this step matters more than it looks.
  2. Seal the weak spots. We apply Henry #923 Butter Grade, a thick, paste-like sealant, to the places roofs leak most often:
    • Seams (where two sections of roofing overlap): 2 inches on each side
    • Penetrations (anything that comes up through the roof, like pipes and vents): 4 inches up and around
    • Exposed fasteners (screws and nails): fully covered and sealed
  3. Apply the base coat. Henry #294 Basecoat at 1.25 gallons per roofing square (1 square = 100 sq ft).
    Why it matters: it helps the top coat grip the roof and stops old stains from bleeding through.
  4. Apply the top coat. Henry #988 Silicone Coating over the entire roof at 2 gallons per square, the amount the manufacturer requires for the {{ bid.warranty_years }}-year warranty.
    Why it matters: this is the waterproof layer you'll see. Its bright white surface reflects sunlight, which helps keep the building cooler.

Manufacturer's Warranty ({{ bid.warranty_years }} years)

Henry Company (a Carlisle Company) warrants the materials against manufacturing defects for {{ bid.warranty_years }} years. If a material turns out to be defective, this warranty also pays for the labor to fix it. Henry issues the warranty directly to you, and you'll receive the full written terms when the project is finished.

Our Workmanship Warranty (2 years)

We guarantee our installation for 2 years from the day we finish. If a problem comes from how we applied the system, we fix it. This warranty doesn't cover severe weather, foot traffic on the roof, damage from other trades, or hidden problems in the roof underneath that couldn't be seen before coating.

If You Want to Change Something

If you choose different materials or add services not listed here, their cost is added to the total price. If an option requires a Preventative Maintenance Agreement (scheduled check-ups with us), that cost is added too.

Your "Material Plus" Warranty: Materials and Labor Covered

Many roof coatings come with a "materials-only" warranty. That sounds fine until something fails. The manufacturer sends a new bucket of coating, and you pay for the labor to remove the failed system and install a new one. That labor bill is often bigger than the cost of the materials.

Your warranty works differently.

This project includes a "Material Plus" Warranty. For the full {{ bid.warranty_years }} years, it covers both the materials and the labor needed to install them.

If a material is found to be defective, the warranty covers:

  • The replacement materials
  • All associated labor costs
  • The entire process of restoring your roof to a watertight condition

Your out-of-pocket cost for a material defect: zero. Henry provides this coverage because we install their system exactly to their specifications, so you get the full protection you're paying for.

Please note: The "Material Plus" warranty covers problems with the product itself. Problems with our installation are covered separately by our 2-Year Workmanship Warranty.
