Coating — {{ bid.warranty_years }} Year System Spec

  • Power wash existing roof system to remove dirt and debris in preparation for the coating application.
  • Apply Henry #923 Butter Grade to seams, penetrations and exposed fasteners
    • Seam: 2 inches to the left and right of seam
    • Penetrations: 4 inches above and around seam

  • Apply Henry #294 Basecoat at a rate of 1.25 gal per square — for proper adhesion & bleed-blocking properties

The Silicone Roof Pros "Material Plus" Warranty: Complete Protection, Zero Hidden Costs

Many roof coatings come with a manufacturer's "materials-only" warranty. There's a critical flaw with this: if the product fails, the warranty may only cover the cost of a replacement bucket of coating. You, the property owner, would still be responsible for the enormous labor cost of removing the failed system and reinstalling the new one—a bill that can often exceed the cost of the materials themselves.

We don't believe in half-measures.

Our standard offering includes a "Material Plus" Warranty. This means for the entire warranty period, we guarantee that both the materials we use and the labor required to install them will perform as promised. If a material we supply is found to be defective, we cover it all: the replacement materials, all associated labor costs, and the entire process of restoring your roof to a watertight condition.

Your out-of-pocket cost for a material defect is zero. This warranty is provided through the manufacturer on systems applied precisely to their specifications, ensuring you get the full protection you pay for.

Please note: This "Material Plus" warranty specifically covers issues with the product itself. Your project is also protected by our separate 2-Year Workmanship Warranty, which covers the quality of our installation.
