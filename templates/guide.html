1. Getting started

  • Start the app: double-click start.bat (Windows) or run ./start.sh (Mac/Linux). First run installs everything; after that it starts in seconds.
  • On this computer: open http://localhost:8000.
  • On your phone/tablet: the startup window prints an address like http://192.168.1.23:8000 — type it into your phone's browser (must be on the same Wi-Fi). Then use Add to Home Screen so it opens like an app with its own icon.
  • One-time setup: on the Templates page, check "Your Info" (name, title, company, phone, email, address) — it fills into every script and invoice. On the Import page, set the off-machine backup folder.
  • Keep the computer awake while you work from your phone — if it sleeps, the app is unreachable until it wakes.

2. The daily routine

  1. Open the Dashboard. The top row tells you today's workload at a glance.
  2. Hit ▶ Work the Queue and go task by task: script on screen, call/email/text with one tap, log it, next.
  3. Chase money: any invoice in the 💰 card is waiting on payment — overdue ones are red.
  4. Glance at 🥶 Going Stale and give each one a follow-up date so it re-enters the flow.
Everything in this CRM flows from one habit: log every touch. Logged interactions clear reminders, feed the timeline, drive Insights, and keep deals from going stale.

3. Dashboard

  • 🎯 / 📅 toggle (top right) — choose whether today's work is ordered by matching buildings or by due date. See Priority.
  • 📌 Due Reminders — cold-cadence steps due today (or overdue), with each account's 🎯 matching-building count. Each row: 📄 Script opens the personalized script, ✓ Log records the step (clears the reminder), ✕ Skip clears without logging. A reminder stays pinned until you do one of those.
  • 💰 Outstanding Invoices — every sent, unpaid invoice; overdue first in red; ✓ Mark Paid on the spot.
  • 📅 Follow-Ups Due — accounts whose follow-up date has arrived, with the note you left yourself, snooze buttons (+1d/+3d/+1w) and ✓ Done.
  • 🥶 Going Stale — pipeline deals with no logged touch in 14+ days and no follow-up scheduled. One tap sets "follow up today."
  • 🎯 Top Priority Accounts — your highest-potential live accounts by matching buildings.
  • 🕘 Recent Activity — your last 10 logged interactions.

4. Work the Queue

One task at a time, combining cadence steps and follow-ups in your chosen order (🎯 Priority by default — biggest matching portfolios first; see Priority). For a cadence task you get the account's info, contact buttons, and every matching script (call script + voicemail + text together for call days) with Copy buttons and one-tap Open in Email app / Dial / Open in Messages. Then:

  • ✓ Log … & Next — records the step and advances.
  • ✕ Skip this step — clears the step without logging it.
  • Skip for now → — leaves the task in place and shows the next one.

Follow-up tasks show your note, a quick log form, snooze buttons, and ✓ Done.

5. Importing lists (CoStar etc.)

  • Upload .xlsx or .csv on the Import page. Columns are matched by meaning, not name or position — "Company", "Account Name", "Cell #", "# Properties (in search)", "Office Phone Number" all work. The summary shows exactly how each column mapped; ignored columns are listed.
  • Pacing: set "Start N /business day" (e.g. 25) so a big list becomes a steady daily routine instead of hundreds of Day-1 tasks. Forgot? Use Re-Pace Cadence — it re-staggers all untouched in-cadence accounts and never moves anyone you've already contacted.
  • Safe re-uploads: duplicate companies are skipped, and missing property counts are backfilled onto existing accounts.
  • Imported accounts capture address/city/zip/website into Notes, the criteria-matching property count (🎯), and total properties owned. Defaults: Prospecting / None-In Cadence → straight into the cadence.

6. Priority: biggest portfolios first

Accounts carry two property counts from your CoStar import: 🎯 matching (buildings that fit your criteria — the "# Properties (in search)" column) and total owned. The matching count is the money number, so the CRM sorts your work by it.

  • Dashboard toggle: 🎯 Priority (default) works the accounts with the most matching buildings first; 📅 Due date is the classic oldest-first order. Your choice is saved and also drives Work the Queue, so the biggest opportunities get your freshest hours.
  • 🎯 column on every due reminder, follow-up and stale row, plus a Buildings in today's tasks tile totalling the opportunity sitting in your queue right now.
  • 🎯 Top Priority Accounts card lists your eight highest-potential live accounts (matching count, total owned, status, last touch) — the shortlist worth extra effort.
  • Accounts page: sort by 🎯 Most buildings (default), and filter with 🎯 ≥ N to see only accounts above a threshold — e.g. type 10 to work only double-digit portfolios. The header badge totals the matching buildings in the current view.
  • Scripts already cite this number: {matching_properties} is what Email 1 means by "roughly N properties built before the 1980s."
A prospect with 40 matching buildings is worth many calls; one with a single building is worth one email. Priority order makes that automatic instead of something you have to remember.

  • Applies only while an account is Prospecting + None / In Cadence. Change either (interested, not interested, any milestone) and all its reminders vanish instantly; change back and they reappear — nothing is ever stored stale.
  • Each account page shows its cadence progress (✅ logged, ⏭️ skipped, 🔴 due, ⚪ upcoming).
  • ↻ Restart Cadence (account page) resets the 10-day clock to today for re-engaging a cold prospect — old cadence steps are kept in history as notes.

8. Contacts & ZoomInfo

  • Each account has a primary contact (fills scripts, emails, invoices) plus unlimited additional people.
  • On an account page: 🔎 ZoomInfo / in LinkedIn buttons jump to research for that company; the + Add Contact form is right below. The first person you add becomes primary automatically; ★ promotes anyone else to primary (the old primary moves down, nothing is lost).
  • Bulk import: upload a ZoomInfo contact export on the Import page. People are matched to accounts by company name (LLC/Inc/punctuation ignored). First person on an empty account becomes primary — their direct dial replaces the company main line, which is preserved in Notes. Duplicates skipped; unmatched companies reported.

9. Scripts & templates

  • Your cold call script, voicemail, text, Email 1, Email 2, and breakup email live on the Templates page — fully editable, with checkboxes controlling which cadence steps each appears for.
  • Placeholders fill from each account when a script opens: {first_name} {last_name} {title} {company} {matching_properties} (fit your pre-1980s criteria) {num_properties} (total) {my_name} {my_company} {my_phone}.
  • Missing info renders as a bracketed hint like [first name] with a warning — nothing half-personalized slips out unnoticed.
  • Open in Email app pre-fills recipient, subject and body — you just press Send. Calls dial; texts open Messages pre-typed.

10. Follow-ups & stale deals

  • Set a follow-up date + note on any account (quick buttons: Tomorrow / +3 days / +1 week). Due follow-ups pin to the Dashboard and Queue until done or snoozed — this is how warm prospects outside the cold cadence stay visible.
  • Pipeline deals with no touch in 14+ days and no follow-up set get flagged 🥶 on the Dashboard.

11. Pipeline board

A column per milestone (Accepted Meeting → … → Closed Won/Lost), each deal as a card showing the contact, last touch, and any follow-up date. The dropdown on each card moves the deal to another milestone right from the board.

12. Roof reports / bids

  • On an account page, 📑 Roof Reports / Bids → + New Report (the address auto-fills from the imported notes).
  • Fill in roof size, non-coated deductions, surface type, warranty years, price, assessment date, and observations (one per line — they become the report's bullet list).
  • Photos: upload straight from your phone — auto-resized, auto-rotated, converted to compact JPEGs. Captions print beside each photo in the two-per-row survey grid.
  • 📑 Open Report generates the full branded proposal in plain English, matching your current template: cover, CEO letter personalized to the contact, the four-step project journey plus the three-phase on-roof work table, roof facts, site assessment + photo survey, a Your Project At A Glance summary box, the coating steps with a "why it matters" line on each, a materials quote computed from the square footage (1.25 gal/sq basecoat, 2 gal/sq topcoat, +5% waste, rounded to 5-gal pails), Your Investment in words and figures, both warranties plus Material Plus, next steps, and the acceptance/signature page. Print / Save as PDF from the browser.
  • 🧮 Warranty Calculator (built in): pick the coating system (Silicone / Acrylic / Aluminum), the roof category (Capsheet / Single-Ply / Sprayfoam / Metal) and the warranty length, and the report quotes the manufacturer's real application rates for that exact combination — e.g. silicone on capsheet is 2 gal/square of topcoat for 10 years but 2.5 for 15 and 3 for 20. Add linear feet for butter-grade mastic, waste/stretch %, and flag a failed adhesion test or rust to add primer. The editor shows the full materials plan (rates, gallons, pails) plus a 10/15/20-year comparison so you can price options.
  • Warranty length flows through the whole document — set it to 10 and every mention (glance box, top coat spec, warranty section) says 10 years.

13. Projects & invoicing (after Closed Won)

  • Move a deal to Closed Won and its account page offers Create Project (the Projects page also lists won deals missing one).
  • Every project starts with the standard job checklist — contract → deposit → materials → crew → job → walkthrough → final payment → warranty. Tap to check off (timestamped), add or remove steps, watch the progress bar.
  • Invoices: add as Draft → Mark Sent (stamps the date; due date defaults to +30 days) → ✓ Paid. Sent invoices pin to the Dashboard until paid; past-due ones show Overdue.
  • 🖨 on any invoice row prints a branded invoice — logo, your info, bill-to with the customer's address, PAID/OVERDUE stamps, and your payment terms (late fee, card fee, lien waivers) in the footer. Print or save as PDF to email.
  • Money rolls up everywhere: per project, across the Projects page, and on Insights (contracted / invoiced / collected / outstanding).

14. Insights

  • Tiles: tasks due, touches this week (with vs-last-week arrow — your effort meter), active prospects, in-cadence pool, closed won, win rate — plus a money row once projects exist.
  • Weekly activity: interactions logged per week, last 8 weeks. The one chart to watch — activity is the number you control.
  • Pipeline: where deals pile up. Cadence reach: how many accounts got each step — a steep drop from emails to calls means calls are being skipped. Status breakdown and activity mix round it out.

15. Data safety

  • Your whole CRM is one file, crm.db. Bid photos live in the uploads/ folder next to it.
  • A dated backup is saved to backups/ automatically once a day (even if the app runs for weeks), newest 14 kept.
  • Off-machine: set the backup folder on the Import page to a synced folder (OneDrive/Google Drive/Dropbox) and every daily backup is mirrored there. Download Full Backup grabs a snapshot from any device.
  • Restore: stop the app, copy a backup file over crm.db, start again.
  • CSV exports of accounts and interaction history are on the Import page.

16. Troubleshooting

  • Phone can't reach the app: same Wi-Fi network? Computer awake? Windows Firewall allowed on private networks? (Re-run the app and accept the firewall prompt, or allow inbound port 8000.)
  • iPhone photos won't upload: they're likely HEIC. Settings → Camera → Formats → Most Compatible, or share as JPEG.
  • Import mapped a column wrong: the import summary shows every mapping — rename the header in Excel to something plainer (e.g. "Company Name") and re-upload; duplicates are skipped so it's safe.
  • Wrong "Your Info" on scripts/invoices: fix it once on the Templates page.
  • Health check: python3 tests/test_crm.py runs the app's 178-check test suite against a throwaway database.
