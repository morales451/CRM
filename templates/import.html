Import Contacts (ZoomInfo export)

Export contacts from ZoomInfo (or any list with Company Name + First/Last Name columns) and upload it here. Each person is matched to your existing accounts by company name — legal suffixes like LLC/Inc/LP/REIT are ignored when matching. The first person added to an account becomes its primary contact; more people stack under the account's Contacts list. Duplicate people are skipped, so re-uploads are safe. Archived companies are never touched.

Picked up automatically from a stock ZoomInfo export: Company Name, First/Last Name, Job Title, Email Address, Direct Phone Number, Mobile phone, LinkedIn Contact Profile URL and Management Level.

Re-Pace Cadence

Imported a big list without pacing and now face a wall of Day-1 tasks? This re-staggers the cadence start dates of all untouched in-cadence accounts (no interactions logged, no steps checked off) at N per business day starting tomorrow. Accounts you've already contacted keep their dates.

Pick N from the number of touches you can do in a day, divided by four. Each account brings five touches over ten days, so new starts stack up: at 25 a day you'd settle at around 100 tasks a day, more on Mondays. If you can manage 40 a day, start 10.
