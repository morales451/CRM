Import Contacts (ZoomInfo export)

Upload a ZoomInfo contact export as it comes — no editing. Each person is matched to your accounts by company name (LLC/Inc/LP and the like are ignored), and when the name doesn't match, by web domain: the export's Email Domain/Website against each account's website. The first person on an account becomes its primary contact; the rest stack under Contacts. Accounts in Research start their cadence. Duplicate people are skipped, so re-uploads are safe, archived companies are never touched, and the whole upload can be undone. Get the company list to search in ZoomInfo →

Picked up automatically from a stock ZoomInfo export: Company Name, First/Last Name, Job Title, Email Address, Direct Phone Number (extensions included), Mobile phone, LinkedIn Contact Profile URL, Management Level, Website, Email Domain and Company HQ Phone (dialed when a person has no direct line).

Re-Pace Cadence

Imported a big list without pacing and now face a wall of Day-1 tasks? This re-staggers the cadence start dates of all untouched in-cadence accounts (no interactions logged, no steps checked off) at N per business day starting tomorrow. Accounts you've already contacted keep their dates.

Pick N from the number of touches you can do in a day, divided by five. Each account brings five touches over ten business days, so new starts stack up: at 10 a day you settle at exactly 50 tasks a day, every working day. If you can manage 50 a day, start 10.
